# Telangana AI–PrajaVani

## Day 1 — Problem Framing

### Problem Statement

Citizens often face difficulty in identifying the correct department, category, urgency level, and appropriate action for their grievances. Government grievance systems may receive complaints containing incomplete, unstructured, or unclear information.

Telangana AI–PrajaVani aims to use AI to understand citizen complaints, classify them into the appropriate department and category, identify urgency, detect possible duplicate complaints, and support better grievance routing.

### Key Problem Areas

1. Citizens may not know which government department is responsible for their issue.
2. Complaints are often submitted as unstructured text.
3. Similar or duplicate complaints may be submitted multiple times.
4. Urgent complaints need to be identified and prioritized.
5. Government schemes and policies need to be connected to relevant citizen complaints.
6. The system should provide consistent and structured information for downstream processing.

### Proposed AI Approach

The system will process a citizen grievance and extract useful structured information such as:

- Department
- Category
- Urgency
- Location
- Complaint information
- Duplicate indicators
- Relevant government scheme or policy information

The structured output can then be used for department routing, prioritization, duplicate detection, and knowledge-based assistance.

### Day 1 Outcome

The problem was converted from a general grievance-management requirement into specific AI tasks that can be represented as structured data and implemented in later stages of the project.
