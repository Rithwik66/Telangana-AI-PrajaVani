# Telangana AI–PrajaVani

## Day 1 — Objectives

### Primary Objective

To define a clear AI-based approach for understanding and processing citizen grievances received through the Telangana PrajaVani system.

### Specific Objectives

1. **Understand Citizen Complaints**
   - Process unstructured citizen grievance text.
   - Identify the main issue described by the citizen.

2. **Department Classification**
   - Identify the government department most relevant to the complaint.
   - Support accurate routing of grievances.

3. **Category Classification**
   - Assign each grievance to an appropriate category based on its subject and issue.

4. **Urgency Identification**
   - Identify complaints that require higher priority.
   - Support prioritization of urgent grievances.

5. **Duplicate Detection**
   - Identify complaints that may represent the same or a very similar issue.
   - Reduce unnecessary duplication in grievance processing.

6. **Structured Data Extraction**
   - Convert important information from unstructured complaints into structured fields.
   - Prepare the data for downstream AI processing.

7. **Government Information Linking**
   - Connect relevant complaints with government schemes, policies, and public documents where applicable.

### Expected Outcome

The Day 1 objectives establish the foundation for building an AI-assisted grievance understanding and routing system. These objectives will guide the dataset design, document corpus, API structure, and later AI implementation.
