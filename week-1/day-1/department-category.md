# Telangana AI–PrajaVani

## Day 1 — Department and Category

### Purpose

Department and category classification helps route citizen grievances to the appropriate government authority. The AI system should identify the department and issue category from the citizen's complaint.

### Department Classification

A grievance should be mapped to the most relevant government department based on the issue described by the citizen.

Examples of departments include:

- Agriculture
- Revenue
- Municipal Administration
- Rural Development
- Health
- Education
- Transport
- Electricity
- Water Supply
- Other relevant government departments

### Category Classification

After identifying the department, the complaint should be assigned to a suitable category describing the nature of the issue.

Examples include:

- Scheme or benefit
- Certificate or document
- Infrastructure
- Service delivery
- Payment or financial issue
- Land or property
- Public services
- Roads and transportation
- Water and sanitation
- Other relevant categories

### Classification Flow

Citizen Complaint
        ↓
Understand Complaint
        ↓
Identify Department
        ↓
Identify Category
        ↓
Route to Appropriate Authority

### Expected Outcome

The department and category fields provide a consistent structure for grievance routing and help prepare citizen complaints for later AI-based classification and processing.
